Measured by `score_box_bias.py` over the 31 leaves Sir marked **corrected** between 7 and 8 September
(`plates/gold-layout.json`). Every claim box is carried through its own leaf's skew and dewarp before
subtraction, so **levelling is never counted as a correction**. Numbers are fractions of page height
or width; negative means the edge moved up or left.

## The systematic error: my boxes sit low

Median edge shift across all 391: **y0 −0.0071 · y1 −0.0048 · x0 −0.0013 · x1 ±0.0000**.

Horizontal placement is essentially right. Vertically every edge moves **up**, the top edge twice as
far as the bottom, so the median box is both raised and slightly shortened (height −0.0051). At 545 ppi
that top edge is roughly **30 px**, about half a line of body type. The likely cause is that I bound a
block from its *ink* while Sir bounds it from its *type body*: ascenders and the leading above the
first line belong to the block, and I was cutting them off.

### RL is two different mistakes

Its mean is outlier-driven; do not quote ×1.71 as typical.

- **Horizontal rules (the median case).** Claim height 0.005 → Sir 0.0093. He roughly doubles them.
  A 5-thousandths box is under 5 px on the review image: too thin to see or grab. Draw rules at the
  thickness of the inked rule plus its leading, not at the ink alone.
- **Four tall vertical rules** (NT 0254, 0404, 0474, 0562, 0564). I drew boxes 0.83–0.87 of page
  height; Sir cut them to 0.20–0.52. I ran column separators the full measure when they serve only
  part of it.

## What to change, and what not to

**For the director's reading procedure** (move 4, "bound the body"):
1. Bound from the **type body**, not the ink: include the leading above the first line and below the last.
2. A **VN** column ends at the last numeral, not at the body block's foot, and never crosses the measure edge.
3. A **rule** is a target, not a hairline: give it the rule plus its leading. A vertical rule spans only
   the block it divides.
4. **DC** is the initial only; body type set beside it belongs to MT.
5. **SG / CW** include every sort in the formula, the trailing numeral and the leading cross or numeral.

**Not to be done:** no blind shift of the cold-read boxes by these medians. Applying Sir's corrections
back onto the claim would launder the only independent signal in the project and make the next round's
agreement figure meaningless. This is a brief for the *reader*, scored on the next cold read.
